## Night at the Crooked Nose Inn

The party spends the night at the Crooked Nose Inn.

That night every member experiences the same strange dream.

A mysterious creature named Geldar appears before them.

Geldar begs the heroes for help rescuing its missing babies.

The meaning of the dream remains unknown.

---

## Chapter 1 — "Treasure, You Say?"

The next day the party is approached by an elf named Eldan.

Eldan explains that roughly 10 miles from his home in the Spotted Spider Forest, he discovered the remains of an ancient stone structure.

Believing it may contain treasure, he asks the party to investigate.

His proposal:

* Explore the ruins.
* Keep the treasure.
* Pay him a finder's fee.

Initial offer:

20% of all treasure recovered.

Negotiation

The party successfully negotiates.

DC 18 Persuasion Success

New agreement:

15% finder's fee

---

## Eldan's Home

Eldan escorts the party to his family's home.

There they meet his father, **Genlan**

Genlan warns the party that the mission could be extremely dangerous and assures them there is no shame in refusing.

After discussing the risks,

NTV accepts the mission.

## Journey

Eldan leads the party to the mysterious location where he originally discovered an ancient stone wall.

The wall appears to be the entrance to an old dragon tomb.

The party attempts to renegotiate Eldan's fee one final time.

Negotiation fails.

Eldan wishes them luck and returns home.

The heroes continue alone.

---

## Chapter 2 — Entering the Dragon's Tomb

The party discovers a broken wooden entrance leading underground.

Rather than lighting a torch, they choose to enter in complete darkness.

Almost immediately they are ambushed.

---

## Combat Encounter 1

### Enemies
- 3 Rats

### Battle Notes

The darkness makes combat chaotic.

Flash throws one of his daggers blindly into the darkness and loses it.

Maya is nearly killed, dropping to 1 HP.

She drinks her Healing Potion during combat and survives.

Rose fired 10 arrows & avoids taking damage.

The party defeats all three rats.
