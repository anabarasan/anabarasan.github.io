## Previously…

The adventuring team NTV traveled to Goldcrest for the Tournament of Heroes and finished in second place, earning 250 gold pieces. After a mysterious shared dream in which a creature named Geldar pleaded for help rescuing its babies, the party accepted a treasure-hunting job from the elf Eldan. They negotiated his finder’s fee down from 20% to 15%, gathered supplies from his home, and journeyed to the entrance of an ancient Dragon’s Tomb. Entering without a torch, they were ambushed by three rats. Maya barely survived, using her healing potion to stay alive, Flash lost one of his daggers in the darkness, and Rose emerged unscathed. The party has defeated the rats and is now resting before venturing deeper into the tomb.
