# Weapons

Human Fighter
* Sword 
* Shield

Special Ability - Shield Hero - When an ally next to you is hit by an attack, you can take the damage instead.

Elf Wizard
* Magic Missile
* Sleep
* Fire Bolt

Special Ability - Arcane Counter - When an enemy casts a spell, you can immediately cast Magic Missile at that enemy.

Dwarf Cleric
* Mace
* Shield
* Healing Word
* Guiding Bolt
* Sacred Flame

Special Ability - Battle Prayer - When you hit an enemy with an attack, you can heal an ally next to you for 1 HP.

Halfling Rogue
* Shortsword
* Dagger
* Shortbow

Special Ability - Sneaky Strike - When you attack an enemy that is next to one of your allies, you deal extra damage.

Dragonborn Paladin
* Longsword
* Shield
* Divine Smite
* Lay on Hands
* Breath Weapon

Special Ability - Heroic Challenge - When you hit an enemy, you can challenge it. Until the start of your next turn, that enemy has disadvantage when attacking anyone except you.

Tiefling Warlock
* Eldritch Blast
* Hex
* Chill Touch

Special Ability - Dark Bargain - When you miss with a spell attack, you can reroll the attack. You must use the new result.

Goliath Barbarian
* Greataxe
* Rage

Special Ability - Ferocious Warrior - Whenever you are hit, you can shove the monster that hit you, up to 2 squares away from you, in any direction.

Tabaxi Ranger
* Longbow
* Shortswords
* Hunter's Mark

Special Ability - Hunter's Instinct - When an enemy tries to hide or escape, you immediately know which way it went.

Human Bard
* Rapier
* Vicious Mockery
* Healing Word
* Dissonant Whispers

Special Ability - Words of Courage - When an ally fails an attack roll, you can let them reroll it.

Elf Druid
* Shillelagh
* Produce Flame
* Entangle
* Cure Wounds

Special Ability - Nature's Wrath - When an enemy enters your Entangle, you can cause the plants to attack it, dealing damage.

Gnome Monk
* Quarterstaff
* Darts

Special Ability - Lightning Reflexes - When an enemy misses you with a melee attack, you can immediately make an attack against that enemy.

Dragonborn Sorcerer
* Dagger
* Light crossbow
Cantrips
* Fire Bolt
* Ray of Frost
* Mage Hand
Spells
* Magic Missile
* Burning Hands
* Shield

Special Ability - Elemental Surge - When your spell damages an enemy, you can cause the spell to jump to another enemy nearby, dealing damage to it as well.
