# Previously...

The annual Pie Festival was about to begin when the legendary **Golden Apple Pie** mysteriously disappeared.

**Mohini, Kora Steelheart, Kragan "Mountain-Breaker" Ogolakanu, and Gimli** investigated the bakery and discovered strange footprints leading to a hidden tunnel. Kragan even smashed the secret entrance wider so the party could fit through!

Deep underground, the heroes discovered strange clues: half-eaten fish, scorch marks, and a child's drawing of a goblin, a dragon, and a pie.

Following the trail, they found **Nib**, a young goblin, hiding with **Pip**, an adorable baby dragon—and the missing pie.

Nib hadn't stolen the pie for himself. Pip was hungry, and Nib had been trying to care for his new friend.

After a little chaos involving a failed attempt to sneak away with the pie, the heroes chose negotiation over violence. Kragan promised to get Nib more pies, while Gimli shared some of his own pie with Pip.

Then the hideout's roof collapsed.

Pip's mother, a huge copper dragon, arrived. After learning that the adventurers had protected her child, she rewarded them with **dragon scales** for armor or weapons.

The heroes returned the pie, saved the festival, and received **250 gold pieces and free pie for life** from Mayor Bramble.

**The heroes' next adventure awaits...**
