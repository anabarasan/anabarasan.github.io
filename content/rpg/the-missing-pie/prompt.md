```
# Prompt: The Missing Pie

You are an experienced Dungeons & Dragons adventure designer who specializes in beginner-friendly, fast-paced one-shots.

Create a complete D&D 5e adventure designed to run in **30 minutes** over **Microsoft Teams** for **3 to 10 first-time players**.

The DM is inexperienced and prefers running printed adventures rather than improvising. Write the adventure as if you're writing directly for the DM.

## Adventure Premise

The town is preparing for its annual Pie Festival.

The legendary Golden Apple Pie has disappeared.

The players are hired to recover it before the festival begins.

During the investigation they discover that the pie was not stolen by a criminal.

Instead, a young goblin secretly took it to feed an adorable baby dragon hiding beneath the bakery.

Just as the players decide what to do, the baby dragon's mother arrives...

End the adventure on a dramatic cliffhanger that naturally leads into a second session.

## Requirements

Produce a complete adventure containing:

* Adventure summary
* Estimated timeline broken into 5-minute blocks
* Boxed text that the DM can read aloud
* A short description of every NPC
* NPC personalities
* Secrets each NPC knows
* Simple clues that always move the story forward
* Optional clues if players get stuck
* Funny moments
* Opportunities for roleplay
* A simple skill challenge instead of long combat
* Optional simplified combat if desired
* Scaling advice for 3–10 players
* How to keep everyone involved remotely
* Suggested images the DM can show on Teams
* A simple one-page map
* Monster statistics using simplified rules
* Ready-to-read ending narration
* Three different cliffhanger endings

Keep the tone lighthearted, cinematic, humorous, and suitable for coworkers who have never played D&D before.

The final output should be formatted like a professional published adventure with clear headings that the DM can quickly reference while running.

Generate the adventure in sections (Overview → Scene 1 → Scene 2 → Scene 3 → ... → Ending → DM cheat sheet). not in one single Go.

Generate each section as copy pastable MarkDown entries. 

once you are ready we can start with Overview.
```
